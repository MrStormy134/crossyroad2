---
title: Major disruption hits East Croydon rail services
image: /images/uploads/image-3.jpeg
---
Thousands of commuters faced disruption this week after a major radio communication fault caused delays and cancellations through East Croydon station between Tuesday 5 May and Thursday 7 May.

The issue affected several major operators including Southern, Thameslink and Gatwick Express, with some passengers reporting delays of up to 90 minutes during busy travel periods.

Rail officials said the fault impacted communication systems used between drivers and signalling staff, forcing trains to run at reduced speeds and causing knock-on disruption across large parts of the network.

East Croydon, one of the busiest stations in the UK outside central London, saw crowded platforms as commuters waited for updates throughout the disruption. Some services were cancelled entirely while others were diverted or terminated early.

Passengers took to social media to complain about overcrowding, confusion and a lack of information during the delays, particularly during morning and evening rush hour periods.

National Rail advised passengers to check before travelling and warned that delays could continue even after the fault was resolved due to trains and crews being out of position.

The disruption once again highlighted the importance of East Croydon as a major transport hub linking London with Sussex, Surrey and Gatwick Airport.

Normal services were gradually restored by Thursday evening, although minor delays continued into the night.
