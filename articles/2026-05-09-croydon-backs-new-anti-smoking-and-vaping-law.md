---
title: Croydon backs new anti-smoking and vaping law
image: /images/uploads/image.jpeg
---
Croydon Council has welcomed a new national law designed to reduce smoking and youth vaping across the UK.

The proposed Tobacco and Vapes Bill would make it illegal for anyone born after 1 January 2009 to legally buy tobacco products in England. The government says the aim is to gradually phase out smoking for future generations while also tightening rules around vaping aimed at children.

Croydon Council said it supports the plans, warning that vaping among young people has risen sharply in recent years. The council also backed stricter controls on brightly coloured disposable vapes and flavours which officials say are often marketed towards teenagers.

Health leaders argue the law could help reduce future cases of lung disease, cancer and heart problems linked to smoking. According to national NHS data, smoking remains one of the leading preventable causes of death in the UK.

However, some critics argue the law could be difficult to enforce and may increase illegal tobacco sales.

The bill is currently progressing through Parliament and could become law later this year if approved.

Councillors in Croydon said the borough wants to continue promoting healthier lifestyles and protecting children from addiction to nicotine products.
