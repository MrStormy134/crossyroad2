---
title: Reform UK surge reshapes Croydon political landscape
image: /images/uploads/image-2.jpeg
---
Reform UK has made significant gains in Croydon following this year’s local election results, adding to the party’s growing momentum across England.

Political analysts say Croydon is becoming one of London’s key battleground boroughs after strong performances from Reform candidates in several wards. The results reflect wider national trends, with voters increasingly turning away from both Labour and the Conservatives in some areas.

Although Labour and the Conservatives still dominate Croydon politics overall, Reform UK’s rise has raised questions about how future elections in the borough could change.

Some voters said they backed Reform due to concerns over immigration, the economy and dissatisfaction with the two main parties. Others argued the party’s gains were more of a protest vote than a long-term political shift.

Croydon has faced years of financial difficulties, council budget pressures and debates over housing and development, all of which became major issues during the election campaign.

Political experts believe Reform’s growing support could split votes in future elections and make council races in Croydon far less predictable.

The election results come as parties across the UK continue analysing why voter support has shifted so rapidly in many parts of the country.
