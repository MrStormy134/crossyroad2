title: Jason Perry Wins Second Term as Croydon Mayor

---
Jason Perry has been re-elected as Mayor of Croydon after a closely fought election that saw the Conservatives narrowly hold onto the borough’s top political role.

The result was announced overnight following Thursday’s local elections, with Perry finishing ahead of Labour candidate Rowenna Davis by just over 1,100 votes. The election drew significant attention across London, with Croydon seen as one of the most competitive mayoral battlegrounds in the capital. 

Final results:

* Jason Perry (Conservative) — 35,871 votes
* Rowenna Davis (Labour) — 34,758 votes
* Peter Underwood (Green) — 19,404 votes
* Ben Flook (Reform UK) — 14,467 votes
* Richard Howard (Liberal Democrats) — 7,815 votes
* Michael Pusey (Taking the Initiative Party) — 2,597 votes
* Jose Joseph (Independent) — 1,568 votes
* Ben Goldstone (TUSC) — 461 votes 

Speaking after the result, Perry said: “Croydon is back on track — now we need to keep it there.” He also described serving as mayor as “a real privilege” and said he wanted to “finish the job” during his second term. 

Perry first became Croydon’s directly elected mayor in 2022 following the council’s financial crisis and the borough’s switch to a mayoral system. During his campaign, he focused heavily on council finances, regeneration, street cleanliness, and crime reduction. Supporters argue Croydon has become more stable under his leadership, while critics point to rising debt levels and council tax increases during his first term. 

The result means the Conservatives retain control of the mayoralty for another four years, despite strong challenges from Labour, the Greens and Reform UK.
